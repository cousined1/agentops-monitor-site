No third party analytics, marketing, advertising, pixel, or font technology is currently installed. The chat assistant is first party: when you submit a lead it sends the details you provided, including the conversation transcript, to our own /api/leads endpoint, where they are recorded in server logs (see the Privacy Notice).

Categories

Strictly necessary: supports security and remembers your privacy choice. It does not require opt-in.

Analytics: would measure site use and performance. It is off by default and no analytics provider is currently installed.

Preferences: remembers optional interface settings, such as the chat assistant lead details. It is off by default: the chat assistant only writes preference storage after you allow it.

Marketing: would support advertising or cross-site measurement. It is off by default and no marketing provider is currently installed.

Your controls

The site uses the strict prior-consent behavior everywhere because reliable IP-based location data is not available in the browser. Locale and time-zone signals are recorded only as a coarse region label. They never reduce protection.

If Global Privacy Control is active, marketing remains off even if Accept All is selected. You can withdraw or change consent at any time by selecting . Clearing site data removes the saved record and causes the banner to appear again.

Changes

A policy version change invalidates the saved choice and asks for consent again. This inventory will be updated before any new analytics, marketing, chat, or similar technology is enabled.

Privacy questions: privacy@agentopsmonitor.com. See the Privacy Notice for request-log and data-rights information.
