Privacy

Privacy notice

This page covers the pre-launch marketing site at agentopsmonitor.com. The AgentOps Monitor product itself is not yet shipped; this notice applies only to site visits.

What we collect

The site runs no third party analytics, advertising, or tracking scripts today. It hosts a first party chat assistant: when you choose to submit a lead through it, we receive the details you provide (email address, company name) together with the conversation transcript, which we use to respond to your enquiry. The hosting platform may record standard request logs (IP, user agent, request path, response status) for operational security and abuse detection. The site stores your privacy choice in local browser storage so it can respect that choice on later visits.

// Example privacy preference payload
{
  "analytics": false,
  "marketing": false,
  "timestamp": "2026-08-19T00:00:00.000Z"
}

How we use the information

Request logs are used to keep the site available, detect malicious traffic, and investigate incidents. Chat lead submissions (email address, company name, and the conversation transcript) are used to respond to your enquiry and are kept only as long as needed for that purpose. Neither is sold or shared.

Cookies and privacy choices

Optional analytics, preference, and marketing technologies remain off unless you allow them. Global Privacy Control signals keep marketing off. Read the Cookie Policy for the current technology inventory, or to update or withdraw consent.

Data retention

Request log retention follows the hosting platform default. Chat lead submissions currently live in those hosting logs on the same schedule; if a dedicated lead store ships, this notice will state its retention window. The product audit trail, once shipped, will be retained according to the configured policy window in each workspace.

Your rights under GDPR and similar laws

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, California, or another jurisdiction with similar rights, you can request access to or deletion of any personal data held in request logs or chat lead submissions. Contact the address below with the request and, for chat leads, the email address you submitted.
