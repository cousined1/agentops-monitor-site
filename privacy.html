Privacy

Privacy notice

This page covers the pre-launch marketing site at agentopsmonitor.com. The AgentOps Monitor product itself is not yet shipped; this notice applies only to site visits.

What we collect

The static site collects no first party analytics. The hosting platform may record standard request logs (IP, user agent, request path, response status) for operational security and abuse detection. Cookies are not set for tracking. No third party scripts, fonts, or pixels are loaded.

How we use the information

Request logs are used to keep the site available, detect malicious traffic, and investigate incidents. Logs are retained by the hosting platform on its standard schedule and are not sold or shared.

Data retention

Request log retention follows the hosting platform default. The product audit trail, once shipped, will be retained according to the configured policy window in each workspace.

Your rights under GDPR and similar laws

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, California, or another jurisdiction with similar rights, you can request access to or deletion of any personal data held in request logs. Contact the address below with the request and a timestamp or IP range.

Contact

Privacy questions: privacy@agentopsmonitor.com.
