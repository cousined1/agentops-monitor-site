# Reproduction Prompt

Build a premium pre-launch B2B SaaS landing page for an AI-agent observability product using `SITE_SPEC.md` and `product-facts.md` as the source of truth.

Requirements:

- Produce three variants from A2 Tufte data-ink, A3 Financial terminal, and D3 Blueprint.
- Use five in-DOM concept surfaces, no product screenshots or generated photography.
- Keep every concept surface visibly labelled and marked with `data-fidelity="concept"`.
- Use only owner-supplied pricing and sample data recorded in `product-facts.md`.
- Do not claim shipping integrations, customers, partnerships, or compliance certifications.
- Use one CTA verb: Install.
- Use no em dashes.
- Run `validate_site.py` and `validate_saas.py` before judging.
- Judge screenshots at desktop and mobile widths, then promote the strongest compliant variant.
